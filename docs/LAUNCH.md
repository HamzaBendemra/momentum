# Launch kit — drafts, not posted

Positioning: **Turn a goal into today’s outcome—and a record of what changed.**

Lead with a familiar problem: a plan tells you what matters, but often leaves today’s finish and the evidence of progress disconnected. Show one complete loop. Give app users and agent users equally clear entry points. Avoid claiming that a tool produces productivity, career outcomes, or verified impact.

## Reddit — builder / open-source community

**Proposed title:** I’m open-sourcing Momentum: a local goal → outcome → evidence loop, plus three agent skills

I built Momentum to make one transition clearer: a goal is useful only when it helps me choose a finish today—and later explain what actually changed.

The beta has four surfaces: Focus, Proof, Review, and Settings & Data. You can create a campaign and milestone, choose one Now, write a daily outcome, capture evidence, and make a weekly decision. Data stays in the browser; there are complete backups and a separate fictional demo. No account or embedded AI service.

The same method is available as three portable skills: choose an outcome, review the week, and build a sourced accomplishment narrative. They work from notes or selected Markdown context, and return drafts rather than changing app records. Consult the compatibility record for the hosts actually tested in this release.

I’m particularly interested in where the loop is unclear: does “today’s outcome” help you decide, or does it add ceremony? Contributions around onboarding, accessibility, examples and evidence fidelity are welcome.

Repository: [Momentum](https://github.com/HamzaBendemra/momentum). [Fictional demo](https://hamzabendemra.github.io/momentum/#/demo/focus). MIT licensed. I’m the author.

## Reddit — agent community variant

**Proposed title:** Three open skills for choosing an outcome, reviewing evidence and drafting a sourced work narrative

Start with the skills and one difficult example: two notes disagree about whether a venue was confirmed, and an aspirational slide claims a 50% improvement without measurements. The intended behaviour is to preserve the conflict, mark unsupported impact, and propose a useful next decision.

Explain that installation and compatibility results are documented. Mention the optional visual app afterwards. Link the specific skill and synthetic evaluation rather than repeating the full app announcement. Ask for difficult inputs that expose overclaiming or weak trade-offs.

**At posting time:** choose a community that welcomes this kind of contribution, read its current rules and promotion policy, check required flair and disclosure, and ask moderators if the rules require it. Do not cross-post identical text indiscriminately, request votes, or invent usage results. No posts are sent by this kit.

## Tester invitation draft — send only when authorized

“I’m preparing a small open-source beta called Momentum. Would you be willing to try a fictional project through goal → daily outcome → evidence → weekly decision, or try one of its agent skills? I’m looking for confusion and failures, not a testimonial. About 15 minutes; no personal data needed. It’s fine to decline.”

Target roughly five willing people across the two audiences. Use Discussions for questions, record confirmed defects as Issues, and thank people without quoting them publicly unless they agree. After a week, summarize actual observations, the changes made and the questions still open. Do not schedule or send invitations automatically.
