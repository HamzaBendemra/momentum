# Fictional plan: a field guide to urban trees

All people, work and evidence in this example are invented for demonstration. This is a plan and notes, not an importable backup.

**Campaign:** A field guide to urban trees. Help neighbours recognise five common trees on a short walk.

**Milestones:** Test a five-tree walking route by 4 October 2026 (Now); share a printable first draft by 18 October (Next); revise it from reader feedback by 1 November (Next).

**Constraints:** Two hours today. No layout work until the route is understandable. Saturday and Sunday are rest days.

**Proposed outcome:** A route draft with the five stopping points and directions between them. Optional proof: a dated Markdown draft. Not today: typography and illustrations.

**Fictional notes:** E1, 1 October: a draft lists five stops, with two unclear turns marked for checking. Reference: `route-notes.md`, revision 1. E2, 2 October: a trial walk identified clearer directions for those turns. Reference: `trial-walk.md`. There is no reader usability evidence yet.

**Review decision:** Ask one willing reader to follow the route after the printable draft exists. A smoother route for the author does not yet demonstrate usefulness for a newcomer.

Try the choose-outcome skill with the plan, review-week with E1/E2, and build-narrative with the dated notes. Expected behaviour: distinguish the proposed draft from completed work and flag the missing reader evidence.
